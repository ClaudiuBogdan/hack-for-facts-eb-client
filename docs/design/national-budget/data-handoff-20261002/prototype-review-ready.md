  state budget's years come from ANAF.

### The two variants (distinct hierarchies, one contract)

### Panorama: overview first

1. One sentence.
2. Plan and execution side by side, each labelled with what it is.
3. A comparability line, with the checks behind „De ce".
4. The four budgets of the law, not added.
5. What the money was spent on and where it came from, as the bulletin's line tree.
6. Every law since 2019 on one chart. Each law's own year is the ink line, its forecasts are dashed, and the selected law's are navy.
7. The authorities, with search and a drill-down sheet.
8. Coverage, then one source line.

### Questions and evidence: investigation first

Three tabs:

- **Ce s-a aprobat?** Each figure shows the printed token, the descriptor row, and a „Dovada" with annex, record, field, publication, file, version and SHA-256. The authorities follow.
- **Ce s-a executat?** The bulletin tree, with the printed cell and token. The rail gives file, coverage, status, finality, release ID and hash.
- **Ce s-a schimbat?** Four comparisons, each run through the comparability checks in a rail.

Both variants read the same feature adapter and Zod types and share the URL keys
(`edition`, `target`, `scope`, `credit`, `release`, `authority`, `q`,
`question`, `compare`, plus `demo`).

### Tradeoffs

- **Panorama:** reads in one scroll and is easier for citizens and journalists. Evidence sits one click away (popover, sheet). It is long on a phone (about 7,000 px).
- **Questions:** the evidence and the rules are visible at the point of each figure, which suits analysts. Its first screen holds less, and some answers are one tab away.
- **Middle path:** a panorama with the questions' evidence rail.

### How the handoff's rules are held

| Rule | How the page holds it |
|---|---|
| Edition vs target year | Every plan value says its edition and target year. Forecasts are never merged across laws. |
| Publication status | The laws are labelled „trimisă la Monitorul Oficial". The 2026 data is „Proiect, martie 2026", unreviewed (`PROIECT` chip). |
| Units | Law amounts are converted ×1,000 to lei. Execution values are used as declared. Each table has one unit, named in its header. |
| Hierarchy and credits | Totals come from explicit descriptor rows (`model/descriptors.ts`, design §5.3); rows are never summed. Budget and commitment credits are a toggle, never added together. |
| Consolidation | The four budgets are listed and not added. The BGC has execution only. „Plan" for the BGC says the laws have no consolidated total. |
| Authority identity | Codes are the law's, shown as codes. The drill-down says the ANAF link needs a reviewed code-to-CUI map. There is no name join. |
| Execution periods | Each release is cumulative from 1 January, and the coverage dates are shown. Flows are never derived from releases. Gap months are visible, with their reasons. |
| Comparability | `model/comparability.ts`. Plan vs execution is always „Nu se compară direct" today (the initial law, not final credits): both figures are shown, with no rate and no gap. Execution January–July vs January–December is not comparable either. Law-vs-law comparisons for the same year, and approved year over year, are comparable and show nominal differences. |

### What is real, draft or invented

- **Real** (no mark):
  - the totals of the seven laws for every target year, from `budget.approved_budget_lines`;
  - the first records of Administrația Prezidențială 2025, marked incomplete;
  - the BGC bulletin facts for December 2025 and July 2026;
  - the release calendar: 241 months, 6 gaps.
- **Draft** (`PROIECT`): the March 2026 static extract (state credits, 55 authorities, their economic split).
- **Invented** (`DEMO`, codes `D01`…): the 2025 authority list other than the Presidency. The values are seeded and apportioned to the real 2025 total; the names are real.

The page-wide strip „Date pentru machetă — API în lucru" explains all three marks.

### API requirements still missing (server asks, design.md §4)

1. A reader for `budget.approved_budget_lines` that returns the source fields, the explicit totals (§5.3) and each authority's own total row.
2. The adopted 2026 law and its rectifications, as versions.
3. A „data through" date for the ANAF execution lane.
4. A sector filter on the national time series.
5. A flag for multi-month deltas (ANAF-01).
6. A reader for the execution releases: the index with gaps and reasons, and one release's facts with semantic key, unit, cell, URL and hash.
7. A reviewed authority-code → CUI map, before any plan-vs-ANAF comparison per ministry.

The proposed endpoint names are in `national-budget-page-api.ts`: `budgetEditions`, `budgetApprovedTotals`, `budgetApprovedSeries`, `budgetApprovedAuthorities`, `budgetApprovedAuthorityLines` and `budgetExecutionRelease`. They are proposals only.
