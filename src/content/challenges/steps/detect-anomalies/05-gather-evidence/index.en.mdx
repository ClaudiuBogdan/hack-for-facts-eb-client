# Gather Evidence

You've identified the anomaly. Now you need to build an evidence file.

## Evidence File Structure

### 1. Context
- What locality are you analyzing?
- What time period?
- What's the anomaly in brief?

### 2. Quantitative Data
- Exact amounts involved
- Relevant percentages
- Comparisons with references (other localities, other years)

### 3. Visualizations
- Screenshots from the platform
- Relevant charts or treemaps

### 4. External Sources (optional)
- Local press articles
- Local council decisions
- Court of Accounts reports

## Challenge

1. Complete all 4 sections above for your anomaly
2. Make sure you have at least 3 quantitative data points
3. Take at least one relevant screenshot

> Solid evidence is the foundation of any successful investigation.
