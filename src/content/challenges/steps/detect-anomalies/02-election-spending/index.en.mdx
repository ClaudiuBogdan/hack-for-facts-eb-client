# Election Year Spending

Local elections influence budgets. Let's see how.

## The Electoral Budget Cycle

A classic spending pattern around elections:

- **Year before elections** — visible investments increase (paving, parks)
- **Election year** — social spending grows (aid, subsidies)
- **Year after elections** — spending drops sharply, borrowing appears

## What to Look For

1. **Compare 2019 vs 2020 vs 2021** (the last complete electoral cycle)
2. Track these categories:
   - Infrastructure investments
   - Social spending
   - Goods and services expenses
3. Is there a visible spike in the election year?

> Electoral spending isn't illegal, but it reveals the administration's real priorities.
