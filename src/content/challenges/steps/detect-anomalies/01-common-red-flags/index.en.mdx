# Common Red Flags

Learn to recognize patterns that may indicate problems in public money management.

## Top 5 Red Flags

### 1. Excessive Personnel Costs
If over 60% of the budget goes to salaries, little is left for investments and services. The national average is ~45%.

### 2. Near-Zero Investment
A municipality that doesn't invest in infrastructure gradually deteriorates. Under 10% capital expenditure is a red flag.

### 3. Sudden Increases Without Justification
A category increasing by over 50% year-over-year needs a clear explanation.

### 4. Spending on "Other Services"
Vague categories like "other expenses" or "miscellaneous services" can hide discretionary spending.

### 5. Persistent Deficit
A budget running a deficit year after year indicates either insufficient revenue or excessive spending.

> These signals don't automatically mean corruption. But they deserve investigation.

## Challenge

Check your locality's budget:
1. Calculate the percentage of personnel expenses
2. Check the investment level
3. Look for vague categories with large amounts
4. Did you find any red flags?
