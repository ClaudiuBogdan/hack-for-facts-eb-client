# Pick an Anomaly

It's time to put everything you've learned into practice. First step: pick an anomaly to investigate.

## How to Choose

Review your notes from previous challenges. Look for:

1. **A significant difference** from similar localities
2. **A sudden change** from one year to the next
3. **A suspicious category** with unusually large expenses
4. **An obvious electoral pattern**

## Criteria for a Good Anomaly to Investigate

- It's **quantifiable** — you can express the difference in lei or percentages
- It's **comparable** — you have a reference point (another year, another locality)
- It's **relevant** — it affects a significant amount of the budget

> Choose something that genuinely intrigues you. Curiosity is the best engine for investigation.

## Challenge

1. List 3 possible anomalies from your findings
2. Choose the most promising one
3. Write in one sentence what exactly is unusual
