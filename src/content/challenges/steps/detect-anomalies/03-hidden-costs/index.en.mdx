# Find Hidden Costs

Some expenses aren't where you'd expect. Let's find them.

## Where Costs Hide

### Transfers Between Institutions
Money can be transferred to subordinate institutions (e.g., sports clubs, cultural centers) where expenses are harder to track.

### Inflated "Operating" Expenses
The "goods and services" category can include:
- Consulting at overvalued prices
- Excessive travel
- Generous protocol and representation expenses

### Oversized Investments
Projects with costs well above market average:
- Paving at double prices
- Urban furniture at premium prices
- Excessively expensive feasibility studies

## Detection Techniques

1. **Compare unit prices** — cost per km of road, per m² of building
2. **Follow the transfers** — where does transferred money go?
3. **Check sub-categories** — drill into "goods and services" details

## Challenge

1. Find the "goods and services" category in the budget
2. If sub-categories exist, identify the top 3
3. Compare with a similar locality — are they proportional?
