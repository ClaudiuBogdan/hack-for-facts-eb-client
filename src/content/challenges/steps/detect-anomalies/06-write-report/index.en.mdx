# Write Your Report

The final step: transform your findings into a clear, actionable report.

## Report Structure

### Title
A clear title describing the anomaly:
> *"Personnel expenses of Municipality X increased by 45% in election year 2024"*

### Summary
2-3 sentences describing the main finding.

### Context
Who, what, when, where — the basic facts.

### Evidence
The data collected, visualizations, comparisons.

### Open Questions
What couldn't you find out from the budget alone? What additional information would be needed?

### Conclusion
A balanced assessment of what you discovered.

## Important Rules

- **Be objective** — present facts, not opinions
- **Be precise** — use exact numbers
- **Be balanced** — mention possible explanations too
- **Be constructive** — suggest what information would clarify the situation

## Final Challenge

1. Write your report following the structure above
2. Review it: is it objective? Precise? Balanced?
3. Congratulations — you've completed all 3 challenge modules!
