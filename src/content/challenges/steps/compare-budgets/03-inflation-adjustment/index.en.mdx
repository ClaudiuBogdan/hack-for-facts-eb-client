# Account for Inflation

A 10% budget increase doesn't necessarily mean more real money.

## Why Inflation Matters

If inflation is 8% and the budget grew by 10%, the real increase is only ~2%. Without adjustment, you might think education is getting more money, when in reality it's getting less in real terms.

## How to Adjust

1. **Note the nominal budget** — the amount in the official document
2. **Check inflation** — the annual inflation rate for that period
3. **Calculate real value** — divide by (1 + inflation rate)

> Example: 100 million lei in 2024, with 8% inflation, equals ~92.6 million in 2023 lei.

## Challenge

1. Pick a spending category
2. Compare the nominal amount between two years
3. Adjust for inflation — did it actually grow or shrink in real terms?
4. Repeat for at least 2 categories
