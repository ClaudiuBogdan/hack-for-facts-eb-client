# Spot Major Changes

Now that you have two years to compare, let's find what changed.

## Where to Look

Focus on:

1. **Large variations** — categories that increased or decreased by over 20%
2. **New categories** — expenses that didn't exist last year
3. **Missing categories** — expenses that were eliminated

## Red Flags

- A category increases by 50%+ without obvious explanation
- Personnel expenses growing faster than inflation
- Investments dropping sharply in a non-election year
- A large expense appearing in an unusual category

> Not every big change is suspicious. But every big change deserves an explanation.

## Challenge

1. Compare expenditures by functional categories between two years
2. Identify the 3 categories with the largest percentage variation
3. Can you explain the changes? If not, note them for investigation
