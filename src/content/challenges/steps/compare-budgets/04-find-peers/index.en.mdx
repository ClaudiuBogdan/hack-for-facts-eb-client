# Find Peer Municipalities

For a fair comparison, you need to compare apples to apples.

## What Makes Two Municipalities Comparable?

- **Similar population** — a commune of 5,000 people doesn't compare with a city of 100,000
- **Similar type** — commune with commune, city with city, municipality with municipality
- **Similar region** — economic conditions vary by region
- **Same period** — compare the same budget year

## How to Find Peer Municipalities

1. Identify your locality's type (commune, city, municipality)
2. Note the approximate population
3. Search for 2-3 similarly sized localities from the same county or neighboring counties
4. Open their budgets on the platform

> Comparing similar municipalities reveals who manages money more efficiently.

## Challenge

1. Find 2 localities similar to yours
2. Open their budgets on the platform
