# Draw Conclusions

You've gathered data, compared, and adjusted. Now you need to interpret.

## How to Formulate Conclusions

Organize your findings into 3 categories:

### What Works Well
- Where is the budget balanced?
- Where are investments growing steadily?
- Where are expenses below the peer average?

### What Raises Questions
- Categories with large unexplained variations
- Per-capita spending far above or below average
- Trends that reverse abruptly

### What Needs Investigation
- Clear anomalies that can't be explained from available data
- Significant differences from similar localities
- Suspicious changes in election years

> Your role isn't to accuse, but to ask questions grounded in data.

## Final Challenge

1. Write 3 conclusions about your locality's budget
2. Formulate at least one question that deserves investigation
3. Congratulations — you've completed the comparison module!
