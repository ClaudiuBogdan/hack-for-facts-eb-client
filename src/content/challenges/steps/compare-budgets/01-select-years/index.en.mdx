# Select Years to Compare

Comparing budgets across years is essential for understanding trends.

## Why Comparison Matters

A single year's budget tells you little. But when you compare 3-5 years:
- You see **trends** — is investment in education growing or shrinking?
- You discover **anomalies** — a sudden spending spike in an election year?
- You understand the administration's **real priorities**

## How to Select Years

1. Navigate to your locality's budget
2. Use the period selector to choose the reference year
3. Add a second year for comparison
4. Ideally, compare the last 3 available years

> Election years (2020, 2024) are particularly interesting — spending tends to increase before elections.
