# Per-Capita Comparison

Total expenditures don't tell the whole story. We need to normalize per capita.

## Why Per Capita?

A city of 200,000 people naturally spends more than one of 50,000. But if you divide by population, you can compare:

- **Per-capita spending** = total expenditure ÷ population
- Shows how many lei the municipality spends for each citizen

## How to Compare

1. Use the "per capita" normalization option on the platform
2. Or calculate manually: total expenditure ÷ locality population
3. Compare across the peer municipalities you identified earlier

## What's Normal?

- Per-capita spending ranges from 1,500 lei to 5,000 lei for communes
- Cities tend to have higher per-capita spending (more complex services)
- Large variations between similar localities warrant investigation

## Challenge

1. Calculate per-capita spending for your locality
2. Calculate for the 2 similar localities
3. Are there significant differences? In which categories?
