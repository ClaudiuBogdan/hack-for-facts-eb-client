# Treemap Basics

The treemap is the primary visualization tool on the platform. Let's learn to read it.

## What Is a Treemap?

A treemap shows how a whole is distributed into parts. The bigger a box, the larger the allocated amount.

- **Size** of the box = proportion of the budget
- **Color** = category or year-over-year change
- **Click** on a box = more details

> The treemap lets you see at a glance where most of the money goes.

## How to Read the Treemap

1. **Look at the big picture** — what are the largest boxes?
2. **Compare** — are categories proportional to what you expected?
3. **Explore** — click on a large box to see sub-categories
