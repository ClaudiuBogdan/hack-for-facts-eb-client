# Drill Down into Details

Now that you can read the treemap at a general level, let's dive deeper.

## Navigating In-depth

Each major category contains sub-categories. For example:

**Education** → may contain:
- Preschool education
- Primary and secondary education
- High school education
- Scholarships and school transport

## Investigation Techniques

1. **Click on a category** to see sub-categories
2. **Use the breadcrumb** to navigate back
3. **Switch between revenue and expenditure** to compare
4. **Check the details** — exact amounts, percentages

> The most interesting findings are often at the second or third level of detail.

## Final Challenge

1. Choose the largest expenditure category
2. Drill into sub-categories — what do you find?
3. Identify the single largest individual expenditure item
4. Note what you discovered — you're on the right track!
