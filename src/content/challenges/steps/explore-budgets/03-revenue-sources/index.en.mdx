# Identify Revenue Sources

Where does the municipality's money come from? Let's find out.

## Main Revenue Types

Local revenue falls into several major categories:

- **Own revenue** — local taxes and fees (property tax, land tax, vehicle tax)
- **Shared taxes** — a percentage of income tax collected by the state
- **Allocated sums** — direct transfers from the state budget
- **Subsidies** — earmarked funds (e.g., heating subsidies)

> The more a municipality relies on its own revenue, the more financially independent it is.
