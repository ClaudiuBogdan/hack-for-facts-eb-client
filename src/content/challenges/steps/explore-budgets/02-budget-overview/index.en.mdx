# Understand the Budget Overview

Once you've found your locality's budget, the next step is understanding its overall structure.

## Revenue vs. Expenditure

Every local budget has two main parts:

- **Revenue** — money coming in (taxes, fees, state transfers)
- **Expenditure** — money going out (salaries, investments, public services)

> A balanced budget means revenue covers expenditure. If spending exceeds revenue, the municipality borrows.

## What to Look For

1. **Total revenue** — how much money the municipality has to spend
2. **Total expenditure** — how much it plans to spend
3. **The difference** — whether the budget is surplus or deficit

## Challenge

Open your locality's budget and note:
- Total revenue
- Total expenditure
- The difference between the two
