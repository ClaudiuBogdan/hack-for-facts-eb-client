# Functional Classifications

Functional classification tells us **what** the money is spent on — education, health, roads, etc.

## What Are Functional Classifications?

Every leu spent by the municipality is classified into a domain:

- **Education** — schools, kindergartens, scholarships
- **Health** — hospitals, ambulances
- **Housing & Environment** — water, sewage, sanitation, lighting
- **Transport** — roads, bridges, public transit
- **Social Assistance** — aid, day centers
- **Administration** — running the municipality

> Functional classification answers the question: **"What purpose is the money spent for?"**
