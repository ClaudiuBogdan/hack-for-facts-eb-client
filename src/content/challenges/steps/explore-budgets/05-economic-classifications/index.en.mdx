# Economic Classifications

If functional classification tells us **what** money is spent on, economic classification tells us **how**.

## What Are Economic Classifications?

Economic classification groups expenditures by their nature:

- **Personnel expenses** — salaries, contributions
- **Goods & services** — materials, utilities, current repairs
- **Transfers** — money sent to other institutions
- **Investment projects** — construction, equipment purchases
- **Interest** — cost of loans

> Economic classification answers the question: **"What type of expense is it?"**

## Why It Matters

A municipality spending 80% on salaries and only 5% on investments probably isn't developing. One investing 30%+ is building for the future.
