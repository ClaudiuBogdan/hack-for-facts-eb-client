# Main Creditor and Better 2026 Questions

## What the main creditor means

The city hall can coordinate and finance not just its own apparatus, but also subordinate institutions within the same budget structure. That is why the idea of a `main budget creditor` matters: it is the institution through which you see the whole budget structure, not just a single institution.

Remember: sometimes the city hall total means more than the city hall apparatus alone.

## Aggregated or detailed

The aggregated view shows how much was managed in total through the same main budget creditor. Totals may include subordinate institutions too.

The detailed view separates what the city hall reported directly from what subordinate institutions reported. That does not mean one view is "right" and the other is "wrong". They answer different questions.

## What to ask for 2026

Once you see what is inside the total and what becomes separate in detailed mode, you can ask sharper questions for the next budget:

- which domain looks underfunded when you look only at the city hall, and what changes when you include subordinates too?
- which institution or spending area deserves separate monitoring in 2026?
- where does the difference between aggregated and detailed materially change your conclusion about priorities?

At that point, you are no longer asking only "why doesn't the city hall do more?". You are asking who is inside the total, what was reported separately, and where the next round of questions should be split by institution.

<ExpandableHint label="A useful caveat when working with aggregated totals">
- The platform tries to reduce the risk of internal-transfer double counting, but consolidation is not guaranteed to be perfect in every case.
- If you need an official confirmation for an exact amount, the safest option is still to check the source document and ask the institution directly.
</ExpandableHint>
