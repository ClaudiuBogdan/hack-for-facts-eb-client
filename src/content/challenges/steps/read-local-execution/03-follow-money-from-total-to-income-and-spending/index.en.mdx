# From Total to Chapters

## From one big number to structure

Budgets and execution reports follow a cascade logic. You first see the total, then the big chapters, then the subchapters, then more detailed rows.

This means the same amount can appear in multiple places without being a real duplication. Most of the time, repetition simply shows the move from overview to detail.

<BudgetCodeAnatomy />

## How chapter codes branch out

A two-digit chapter code like **65** (Education) is not one flat line. It splits into subchapters (65.03, 65.04...) and then into paragraphs (65.03.01, 65.03.02...). Each level adds precision: the chapter is the topic, the subchapter narrows the scope, and the paragraph identifies the exact programme or activity.

The tree shows this branching for Education. Every spending or revenue chapter in a local budget follows the same pattern.
