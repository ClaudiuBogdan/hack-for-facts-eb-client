# Functional and Economic Lenses

## Two different questions

For spending, the same amount can be read in two ways:

- functional: which public domain receives the money?
- economic: what type of spending is it?

The first lens helps you see whether the money goes to education, transport,
environment, or administration. The second lens shows whether the spending is
about salaries, goods and services, social assistance, or investment.

## How the two codes combine

A school modernization project can be read like this:

- functional: `65` or a more detailed code under education
- economic: `71` or another capital / non-financial asset code

The same logic works for current spending:

- if you see `65` + `10`, you are looking at education + personnel spending
- if you see `84` + `20`, you are looking at transport + goods and services

As a practical rule:

- `operation` or `functioning` keeps the service running
- `development` changes its infrastructure, capacity, or quality
