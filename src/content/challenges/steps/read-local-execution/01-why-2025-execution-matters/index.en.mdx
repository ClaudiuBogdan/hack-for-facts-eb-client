# Why 2025 Execution Matters

## Why we start from execution

If you want to participate better in the 2026 local budget debate, the best starting point is the 2025 execution. That is where you see what money actually came in and what money was actually spent, not only what was promised.

The approved budget shows intention. Execution shows reality. That is why the 2025 execution is such a useful base for asking better questions about 2026.

## What you can already read on the selected city hall page

The selected city hall page already gives you several strong entry points:

- total income
- total spending
- budget balance
- multi-year trends
- category breakdowns
- official reports

In this module we start with the aggregated view because it gives the clearest overall picture of the money managed through the city hall and its funding structure.

## Why we start with the aggregated view

At the beginning, the most important thing is to see the big picture: how much money was managed in total, which domains weigh the most, and how things changed over time. The aggregated view is the best first reading for that.

Later, you will also see why it is sometimes worth switching to detailed execution, especially when you want to separate the city hall apparatus from subordinate institutions.

<ExpandableHint label="Useful notes about the data on the page">
- The data comes from budget execution reports published through the Ministry of Finance / ANAF portal.
- Per-capita calculations use the resident population based on the 2021 census as a common benchmark.
- In aggregated mode, totals may include both the city hall and subordinate institutions financed through the same main creditor.
</ExpandableHint>

## Your task for this step

In this first step, the totals stay hidden. The goal is only to understand why execution is the right starting point and what kind of data you will read in the next lesson, where the first real total is revealed.
